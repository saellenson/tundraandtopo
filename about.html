About

I'm a Senior Geospatial Analyst and cartographer at Dewberry, based in Anchorage, Alaska. My work spans GIS web development, remote sensing, and Arctic and wetland mapping — turning raw geographic data into something a person can actually read.

I hold an M.S. in Geography from the University of Montana, where my thesis used photogrammetry and LiDAR to study tundra vegetation change on Alaska's North Slope.
